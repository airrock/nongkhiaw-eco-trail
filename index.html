    Why “Eco-Trail”

    Sharing our home, our culture and our nature.

    Nong Khiaw Eco-Trail is run by a local team from Nong Khiaw and the surrounding villages. We have been organizing local tours since 2024, sharing our home, culture and nature with travelers.

    We call it “Eco-Trail” because we focus on responsible travel, supporting local communities and protecting the natural environment.

    How to book

    Book in 7 simple steps

    It is easy. Follow these steps on this website.

    1. 1

      Visit our website

      Go to nongkhiawlaos.com on your phone or computer.

    2. 2

      Browse our tours

      Look at the tours above and choose the one you like.

    3. 3

      Click “Book this tour”

      Press the button on your chosen tour. It takes you to the booking form.

    4. 4

      Fill in the booking form

      Add the date, the number of people, and each traveller’s name and passport details. You can also add a pickup and a takeaway lunch.

    5. 5

      Check and send

      Check your details and press the send button. WhatsApp opens with your booking ready to send.

    6. 6

      We confirm your booking

      We reply on WhatsApp to confirm your tour and arrange the details.

    7. 7

      Pay when you arrive

      Pay at our office in Nong Khiaw, in the way you chose in the form (cash, card or BCEL QR).

    8. Need help? Message us on WhatsApp.

    Good to know

    Quick answers

    How much do the tours cost?

    USD 30 per person.

    What time does it start?

    The day starts at 8:30 am with a scenic boat ride upstream on the Nam Ou.

    What is included?

    An English-speaking guide, entrance fee tickets to sightseeing, 2 bottles of drinking water and lunch.

    Can I eat vegetarian?

    Yes, vegetarian options are available for the picnic lunch.

    What should I bring?

    Mosquito repellent, comfortable shoes, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, toiletries, a swimming suit and a change of clothes.

    How do I book?

    Message us on WhatsApp at +856 20 5897 5057, or use the form below and it will open WhatsApp for you.

    Is transport from Luang Prabang included?

    No, but we can arrange it.

    Do I need to bring a tent?

    No.

    Where can I leave my luggage?

    At our office.

    Booking & cancellation policy

    How do I confirm my tour booking?

    Your tour is confirmed once we receive your booking details.

    Can I get a refund if I cancel my tour?

    No refund applies for personal reasons.

    Can I change my tour date?

    Yes, depending on availability.

    What happens if the weather is unsafe for the tour?

    We will arrange an alternative date.

    Thank you for your understanding and for supporting sustainable local tourism in Nong Khiaw, Laos.
